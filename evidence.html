Engineering judgment

Decisions and tradeoffs.

AskCV · context

Use the complete CV

A small, curated source fits in the prompt. This avoids retrieval ranking and another service, while making corpus size and synchronized deployments ongoing constraints. Reconsider retrieval when the source grows or needs selective access.

AskCV · validation

Derive allowed labels

A historical test exposed a fixed citation cap that rejected an answer citing six valid sections. The limit was aligned with the allowed source set. Validating a label still does not establish entailment.

ManeMap · reliability

Limit model-service waits

The architecture describes bounded timeouts, retries and cancellation. These are controls in the implementation, not a published availability or latency measurement.
